Yechan Noh won **Outstanding Public Engagement Through Research Communication** at the 2026 University of Notre Dame Postdoc Spotlight for his talk, **“Beyond Current AI: Mimicking the Brain’s Physics.”**

The award was announced by Notre Dame’s Office of Postdoctoral Affairs in its Postdoc Newsletter.
